Brand & Style
This design system crafts an aura of precision, intelligence, and high-velocity execution for next-generation B2B AI revenue teams. Synthesizing the bespoke tactility of classic editorial publications with the razor-sharp telemetry of hyper-modern sales tech, it communicates effortless mastery over enterprise pipeline chaos.
Aesthetic Personality
Warm Editorial Precision: Rather than relying on sterile, cold grays or stark stark-white interfaces, the environment breathes through warm, creamy canvas surfaces reminiscent of heavy archival stock paired with pitch-black obsidian type.
Ambient Chromatic Radiance: Soft, organic atmospheric glows in lavender, incandescent warm amber, and electric indigo emerge from behind structured product interfaces to evoke synthetic machine cognition operating behind quiet glass.
High-Information Density with Visual Restraint: Complex telemetry—lead waterfall stages, live signal tickers, deal propensity charts, and AI call annotations—is packaged inside disciplined micro-containers with whisper-weight borders.
Emotional Response
The interface must instill profound confidence and executive clarity. Revenue operators and founders should feel as if they are orchestrating their enterprise from a high-end command center that values focus over flash.
Colors
The palette is engineered around an architectural triad: warm archival neutrals for grounds, pitch obsidian for typography, and targeted synthetic signals for high-leverage data states.
Core Architecture
Surface Canvas (`#FBFBF9`): The primary base backdrop. Carries a slight stone-cream undertone that eliminates eye fatigue during continuous operational sessions.
Surface Layer / Panel (`#F7F6F2`): A secondary warm tinted foundation for elevated layout containers, sidebars, and nested dashboard canvases.
Surface Card Highlight (`#FFFFFF`): Reserved for foreground cards, floating popovers, and interactive micro-widgets to establish natural depth over the cream canvas.
Obsidian Dark Surface (`#0D1117`): The deep midnight canvas deployed exclusively for high-impact hero contrast bands, enterprise callout modules, and deep terminal sections.
Structural Strokes
Border Subtle (`#E9E8E3`): The foundational hairline border token used throughout cards, cell dividers, and inline modules.
Border Focused / Active (`#D5D3CB`): A slightly intensified neutral outline for hover states, active inputs, and elevated tabs.
Strategic Accents & Aura Gradients
Deep Obsidian (`#111827`): Primary action buttons, dominant headlines, and assertive structural elements.
Electric Violet / Indigo (`#4F46E5`): Denotes active AI synthesis, algorithmic discovery, and primary interactive highlights.
Warm Amber Flame (`#D97706`): High-priority lead indicators, trigger alerts, and active prospect engagement tags.
Soft Lavender Aura (`#818CF8`): Ambient background blooms and subtle focus rings.
Signal Emerald (`#059669`): High deal velocity, verified email delivery, and positive ARR momentum.
Typography
Plus Jakarta Sans drives the typographic system across all interfaces. Its crisp geometry and open aperture maintain high legibility even in dense telemetry viewports, while its tight negative tracking in larger scales channels a refined editorial cadence.
Display Hierarchies
Large display headings (`display-xl`, `display-lg`) leverage aggressive negative tracking (`-0.035em` to `-0.025em`) and condensed line-heights. This gives marketing headlines and product value claims an authoritative, journalistic punch without looking bulked or unconsidered.
Hero narrative sections pair solid-state black headers (`#111827`) with soft body copy (`#52525B`) to guarantee instant ocular hierarchy.
Micro-Typography & Telemetry
Data labels, live telemetry badges, and entity tags rely on `label-sm` or `caption-caps` in uppercase styling with positive letter-spacing (`0.06em`) to ensure instant differentiation from conversational prose.
Machine-generated queries, prospect email templates, and AI workflow variables employ `code-sm` (`JetBrains Mono`) to reinforce procedural authenticity.
Layout & Spacing
The architecture operates on an 8pt base grid system, stepped down to 4pt for compact component padding and micro-telemetry cards.
Layout Grid & Bounds
Maximum Width Container: The core content container is clamped at `76rem` (1216px), preventing eye strain across ultra-wide desktop monitors and keeping marketing narrative content naturally readable.
Desktop Grid Structure: Standard dashboard views and marketing presentation decks adhere to a 12-column fluid grid system with `2rem` (32px) gutters and a minimum edge margin of `2rem`.
Vertical Rhythm: Macro-sections on marketing landing pages utilize generous breathing room (`6rem` / 96px vertical padding), allowing complex interactive mockups and floating badge clusters to float cleanly over ambient backdrops.
Breakpoint Adaptation
Mobile (< 640px): Single-column stack. Container padding drops to `1rem` (16px). High-density horizontal product tables convert to stacked entity cards. Floating telemetry pills reposition as scrollable horizontal chips.
Tablet (640px - 1024px): 6-column fluid structure. Outer margins adjust to `1.5rem` (24px). Split feature layouts pivot from side-by-side to stacked visual-over-prose formats.
Desktop (1024px+): Full 12-column presentation. Split asymmetric grids (e.g., 5-column copy to 7-column interactive preview frame) anchor the page.
Elevation & Depth
Depth is established through soft, multi-layered diffuse drop shadows and warm tonal layering rather than heavy structural bezels or opaque surface shifts.
Shadow Styles
Surface Elevation 1 (Resting Card): `0 1px 2px 0 rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.03), 0 1px 3px 1px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.02)` overlaid on a pure white `#FFFFFF` surface with a `#E9E8E3` hairline stroke.
Surface Elevation 2 (Hover / Active Card): `0 12px 24px -4px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.05), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.02)`. Used for interactive cards, lead preview tabs, and floating search selectors.
Surface Elevation 3 (Floating Modal / AI Drawer): `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.12), 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.04)`. Accompanied by a 24px backdrop blur (`backdrop-blur-md`) on underlying views.
Chromatic Aura Glows
Ambient atmospheric glows are injected into the background of product preview frames using diffuse radial gradients:
Synthesis Glow: `radial-gradient(circle at 50% 0%, rgba(129, 140, 248, 0.18) 0%, rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.08) 45%, transparent 70%)` with a `blur(60px)` layer filter.
Signal Alert Glow: A focused, low-opacity warm amber wash positioned directly behind live trigger alerts and hot-lead counters.
Shapes
The design system maintains a balanced geometry (`roundedness: 2`), blending crisp modern card corners with fully pill-shaped micro-elements to communicate polish and tactile affordance.
Corner Radii Guidelines
Micro Radii (`0.375rem` / 6px): Checkboxes, nested tag badges within tight data grids, and keyboard shortcut indicators.
Standard UI Radii (`0.5rem` / 8px): Form input fields, dropdown trigger targets, contextual popovers, and table action items.
Container Radii (`1rem` / 16px to `1.5rem` / 24px): Product cards, interactive dashboard mockup frames, and segmented preview panels.
Pill Geometry (`9999px`): Master CTA buttons, top-level announcement pills, status badges, and signal tags.
Components
Buttons
Primary Action (Pill Button): Solid `#111827` background, `#FFFFFF` typography (`label-md`), full pill geometry (`rounded-full`), padded `0.625rem 1.375rem`. Features an ultra-subtle top inset highlight (`box-shadow: inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`). On hover: transforms slightly with `#1F2937` and an elevation lift.
Secondary Action (Warm Ghost Pill): Pure `#FFFFFF` fill enclosed by a `#E9E8E3` border, `#111827` text. On hover: canvas shifts to `#F7F6F2` with border deepening to `#D5D3CB`.
Inline Pill Badge: Compact, high-polish rounded badge used for product updates and announcements (e.g., "New: Duo Copilot is now live"). Border `#E9E8E3`, creamy fill `#FFFFFF`, with a vibrant interior dot or miniature accent tag.
Cards & Mockup Viewports
Stacked Product Frames: Built with a pure `#FFFFFF` interior set over `#F7F6F2` nested canvases. Layered tabs display stacked states (e.g., Intelligence, Deliverability Optimization, Multichannel Engagement). Active tabs present a crisp white elevation over muted receding tabs.
Interactive Deal Telemetry Cards: Micro-cards floating over main dashboard previews. Employs soft drop shadows, left-anchored signal accent markers (e.g., 3px solid vertical bar in `#4F46E5` or `#D97706`), avatar initials, and inline metrics (+78% open rate, 9x ROI signals).
Chips & Signal Tags
Lead Signal Waterfall Tags: Pill-shaped status indicators with low-saturation pastel fills and high-contrast text:
Hiring Intent: `#EEF2FF` fill with `#4338CA` text.
Competitor Churn: `#FEF3C7` fill with `#92400E` text.
C-Suite Promotion: `#ECFDF5` fill with `#065F46` text.
Every tag features a 6px circular status bead on the leading edge with pulsing ambient animation for live, incoming events.
Input Fields & Search Bars
Command Search Bar: Floating pill-shaped input housing `#111827` placeholder text at 40% opacity, an inline leading magnifying glass or AI sparkle glyph, and trailing keyboard hint (`⌘K`). White `#FFFFFF` background with `#E9E8E3` border, expanding slightly on focus with an indigo glow ring (`0 0 0 3px rgba(79, 70, 229, 0.12)`).
Email Capture Bar: Integrated inline split input with an embedded black pill submit button tucked cleanly inside the right boundary of the field.
Lists & Telemetry Tables
Prospect Feed: Dense, highly legible row elements on `#FFFFFF` with alternating hairline dividers (`#F4F4F0`). Columns align strictly with metadata badges, company icons, and real-time status chips. On hover, rows softly shift to `#FBFBF9`.

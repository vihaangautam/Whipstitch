Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our BYOK infrastructure and data guarantees.

How does the BYOK (Bring Your Own Key) model work? +

You enter your own API credentials for Gemini and Groq in the BYOK Key Vault. Whipstitch encrypts them with AES-256 Fernet. We do not mark up model token costs—you pay your LLM provider directly at wholesale pricing, giving you complete visibility and control over cost governance.

What transcript formats does the Deal Health engine support? +

You can upload or stream .txt, .vtt, .srt, .docx, and .pdf call recordings from Zoom, Google Meet, or Microsoft Teams. The engine extracts speaker turns and generates MEDDPICC criteria along with verbatim quotes, blocker evaluations, and automated follow-ups.

How are Apollo credits safeguarded? +

Outbound discovery and inbound waterfall enrichment check your local workspace cache first before calling external endpoints. Hard daily caps and idempotency locks prevent runaway credit usage.

Is customer conversation data stored or used for LLM training? +

Never. We operate under zero-data retention agreements with model providers. Your transcripts and playbooks remain strictly scoped to your private PostgreSQL tenant database.
